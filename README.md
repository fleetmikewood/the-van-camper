
# The Roam Pod 
**Original Concept, Architecture & Prior Art by fleetmikewood (Michael Ray Fleetwood)**

![The Van Camper Outdoor Concept](van-camper-concept.jpg)

## Overview
Why do pickup truck owners get hard-shell slide-in campers while minivan owners are limited to soft canvas pop-up tents? 

**The Roam Pod** solves that engineering gap: a self-contained, hard-sided, insulated rooftop camper shell engineered specifically to mount onto standard minivan factory roof rack points (modeled here on a Dodge Grand Caravan platform)[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span)[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span). It delivers four-season weatherproofing, security, and rigid sleeping quarters in an ultralight footprint designed for a daily-driver minivan—without sacrificing interior passenger seating or cargo space below[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span)[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).

## Preliminary Technical Specifications
* **Shell & Frame:** Molded fiberglass composite exterior shell with an internal aluminum support frame and full thermal insulation[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span)
* **Target Dry Weight:** ~250 lbs[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span)
* **Exterior Dimensions:** 108" (9 ft) Length × 52" Width × 36"–38" Height (closed profile)[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span)
* **Mounting System:** Direct integration with factory minivan roof rack crossbars and mounting points[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span)
* **Interior Layout:**[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span)
  * Forward cab-over sleeping berth[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span)
  * Dual side ventilation windows[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span)
  * Compact, custom recessed storage cubbies[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span)
  * Rear hatch access via removable rear ladder[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span)[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span)
* **Power & Lighting:** Roof-integrated solar panel capability and warm interior LED strip lighting[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span)[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span)
